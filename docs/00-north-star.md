# North Star

Policy Dispute AI Assistant is a research prototype for turning homeowners policy language and denial letters into structured A-G dispute reports.

It is for public adjusters, coverage attorneys, and claim-review professionals who need a faster first-pass triage view before human review. The current app helps organize policy concepts, denial reasons, potential dispute angles, missing information, and confidence notes.

It is not a legal product, not a coverage decision, not attorney advice, and not a substitute for reviewing the actual policy, denial letter, claim file, jurisdiction-specific law, or professional judgment.

The portfolio goal is to show a credible AI-assisted workflow while keeping demo safety, privacy, and not-legal-advice framing visible.
